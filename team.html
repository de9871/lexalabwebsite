Principal Investigator

Yanru Chen, Ph.D., BCBA

Principal Investigator

Assistant Professor of Autism

Dr. Yanru Chen is the principal investigator of the LEXA Lab and an Assistant Professor of Autism in the College of Education and Human Development at Georgia State University. Her research focuses on executive function, language and literacy development, and social communication and engagement in autistic children, including those who are minimally verbal.

After receiving her Ph.D. in Intellectual Disability and Autism at Columbia University, Dr. Chen completed her postdoctoral training at the Center for Autism Research Excellence at Boston University and was later a research assistant professor in the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences.

Dr. Chen has over a decade of experience working with autistic children and their families in both research and school settings as an early childhood special education teacher, a Board Certified Behavior Analyst, and a trained and reliable Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule (ADOS) clinician.

She has published across several top-tier autism journals, including Autism Research, Molecular Autism, and the Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders. Her work was also selected as Featured Research at the International Society for Autism Research Annual Meeting and was spotlighted in the Autism Science Foundation science podcast.
